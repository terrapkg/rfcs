# Terra RFCs

This describes Terra RFCs.

## Why This Needs to Exist

We need more ideas on how Terra can become a better repository for everyone.
By introducing an RFC repository, we encourage anyone interested to provide their opinions.

## The Terra Community

Terra is possible thanks to support from many different people, including our upstream distributions
and upstream software projects. To further improve our transparency and communications, having a
public repository of e.g. what we might do is probably a good idea.
