## Andaman

[anda](https://github.com/FyraLabs/anda/) is our build toolchain for building rpm packages.
It's currently just a thin wrapper around [rpmbuild] and [mock].

Andaman is named after the [Andaman Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andaman_Sea) by Cappy.
(Fedora → Sea reference; Ultramarine Linux Thai origin → Andaman)
